Career Simulator Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Career Simulator does not collect, store or share your personal information. The app is published by Ivan Dyptan.

What the app collects

Nothing. The game runs on your device. Your choices and progress are not sent anywhere, and the app does not save them when you close it. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

What stays on your device

One setting that records whether you have seen the Career Advisor's introduction. It is kept in the app's own settings on your device and never leaves it.

Game Center

If you play a Real Life run and are signed in to Game Center, the game submits your score to the leaderboard for the country you chose. The Leaderboard screen reads that board back from Game Center and shows its ten best scores, with the player names Game Center provides, and your own place. The app shows this only while the screen is open: it does not save it and does not send it anywhere else. It does not store your Game Center name or identifier, or anyone else's.

Game Center is Apple's service: Apple handles the scores and shows each one with the player's Game Center nickname, following the privacy settings of that player's Game Center profile. This is covered by Apple's Game Center privacy information. If you are not signed in, nothing is submitted or shown.

Apple Intelligence

On devices that support it, you can type to the Career Advisor. Your text is processed on your device by Apple's on-device model. The app does not save it and does not send it to us or to any server.

Information from Apple

Apple, not the app, may give the developer anonymous, combined statistics about the App Store listing, such as the number of downloads, and crash reports from people who allowed sharing diagnostics with developers in their device settings. These do not identify you.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub. You need a free GitHub account, and issues are public, so please do not include personal information.
