Career Simulator Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Career Simulator does not collect, store or share your personal information.

What the app collects

Nothing. The game runs on your device. Your choices and progress are not sent anywhere. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

What stays on your device

One preference that records whether you have seen the Career Advisor's introduction. It never leaves your device.

Game Center

If you play a Real Life run and are signed in to Game Center, the game submits your score to the leaderboard for the country you chose. This is handled by Apple's Game Center service and is covered by Apple's Game Center privacy information. The app itself does not read or store your Game Center name or identifier. If you are not signed in, nothing is submitted.

Apple Intelligence

On devices that support it, you can type to the Career Advisor. Your text is processed on your device by Apple's on-device model and is not sent to us or to any server by the app.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
